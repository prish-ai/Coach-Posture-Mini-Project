Most developers don't realise their posture is destroying their spine

Developers sit for 8–10 hours a day staring at screens. Over time the head drifts forward, shoulders round, and the spine compresses. The pain doesn't start immediately — it creeps in after months or years, by which point the damage is already done.

80% of devs get chronic back painAvg onset: 3–5 years into careerMost cases are fully preventable

Every inch forward adds 5kg of pressure — your spine is screaming

Forward head posture is the most common coder injury. When your head moves 2–3 inches forward, your spine takes up to 27kg of extra force. Over years this causes disc compression, nerve damage, and chronic headaches that destroy your productivity.
